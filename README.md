https://zanlib.dev/blog/do-you-still-read-the-code/

## What Is This Article About?

The article talks about two types of developers who use AI to code:

- **Accelerators**, who use AI to help build software while still understanding the implementation.
- **Vibe coders**, who let AI write the code and focus mainly on whether the final output works.

What makes this article especially interesting is its emphasis on **reading code**. Reading code generated by AI is not simply about going through the output. It means understanding how the implementation works, why it was built that way, and how to retain that knowledge for future changes.

The article also introduces two kinds of technical debt that can emerge from the way we work with AI: **Cognitive Debt**, caused by failing to understand the code, and **Intent Debt**, caused by losing sight of the reasoning behind the implementation. These ideas make the article a thoughtful reminder that effective AI-assisted development still depends on human understanding.
